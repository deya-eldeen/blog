Reactive Playground Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Reactive Playground app.

Reactive Playground is designed to work without collecting information from you. The app does not collect, store, share, sell, rent, or transmit personal data or usage data.

Summary

Reactive Playground collects no data at all.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, user accounts, remote servers, or third-party services that collect information from you.

Any work you do in Reactive Playground stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it using features provided by your device or operating system.

Data Collection

Reactive Playground does not collect any personal information, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Files
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Usage history
  • Crash reports
  • Diagnostics
  • Analytics events

The app does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide contact details.

Local Data

Reactive Playground may allow you to create or edit content inside the app. That content remains on your device and is not sent to the developer or to any server controlled by the developer.

If you delete the app, any app data stored locally by the operating system may be removed according to your device’s normal app deletion behavior.

Network Activity

Reactive Playground does not send app usage data, personal data, or analytics data over the network.

The app does not connect to developer-operated servers for tracking, profiling, advertising, analytics, or account management.

Analytics and Tracking

Reactive Playground does not use analytics tools, tracking SDKs, advertising networks, cookies, fingerprinting, or similar technologies.

The developer does not track how you use the app.

Third-Party Services

Reactive Playground does not integrate third-party services that collect data from users.

If you choose to share or export content using operating system features, such as a share sheet or another installed app, that action is handled by the operating system or the app you choose. Their privacy practices are separate from Reactive Playground.

Data Sharing

Reactive Playground shares no data with the developer or with third parties.

Because Reactive Playground does not collect data, there is no user data to sell, rent, disclose, or transfer.

Children’s Privacy

Reactive Playground does not knowingly collect data from children or from any other users.

Because the app collects no data, it does not build profiles, track behavior, or store information about children.

Data Retention

Reactive Playground does not retain user data on developer-controlled systems because it does not collect any user data.

Any content that remains on your device is controlled by your device and operating system.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Reactive Playground changes in a way that affects privacy. If the app ever begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated to explain what is collected, why it is collected, and how it is handled.
