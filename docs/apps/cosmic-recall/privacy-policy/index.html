Cosmic Recall Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Cosmic Recall is a memory game for iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets, made by Deya Elkhawaldeh. This policy explains what information the game uses and where it goes. The short version: I don’t collect, store, or share any of your personal information.

Information I Collect

None. Cosmic Recall has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The game doesn’t send any information to me or to any server I run.

Information Stored on Your Device

To remember your place, the game saves a small file on your device. It contains:

  • your campaign progress (levels completed, last level played, last world viewed) and your best score
  • your settings (sound on or off, game speed, and language)
  • if you use Game Center or Google Play Games, whether to reconnect automatically and any scores waiting to be sent

This file stays on your device. I can’t access it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

Game Center is optional. If you choose to connect, the game uses Apple’s Game Center to sign you in and to send your number of completed levels to the game’s leaderboard. Apple provides and runs this service, and Apple’s Privacy Policy covers it.

Your Game Center nickname and score may be visible to other players on the leaderboard, according to your Game Center settings. You can sign out of Game Center or change those settings any time in your device’s Settings app. You don’t need Game Center to play.

Google Play Games (Android)

Google Play Games is optional. If you’ve used Google Play Games before, it may sign you in automatically when the game starts; otherwise it signs you in only when you choose to connect. Once you’re signed in, the game sends your number of completed levels to the game’s leaderboard. Google provides and runs this service, and Google’s Privacy Policy covers it, including what Google Play Games itself collects.

Your Play Games gamer name, profile picture, and score may be visible to other players on the leaderboard, according to your Play Games profile settings. You can change those settings, or stop using Play Games with this game, any time in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. You don’t need Google Play Games to play.

Device Motion

The game reads your device’s tilt to move the stars in the background slightly. This happens only on your device; the motion data is never saved or sent anywhere.

Children’s Privacy

The game doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and its effective date.
