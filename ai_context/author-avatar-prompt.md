Match his very close 2–3 mm dark buzz cut, fair/light skin with warm undertones, subtly fuller cheeks and jaw, thick eyebrows, brown eyes, large black rounded glasses, neat dark beard and mustache, and friendly slight smile. Keep the CURRENT weight/fullness from Image 1; do not increase or reduce it.
Composition: Only the complete head, including scalp, ears, beard and chin, centered in a square with a small clear margin. Similar three-quarter head angle to the supplied avatar. Fill roughly 85–90% of the square. Keep all edges clean and the face readable at 64 pixels.
Constraints: Actual transparent background with alpha, no white square. No hands, neck, shoulders, clothing, desk, laptop, scene, icons, text, border, badge, watermark or shadow backdrop. Naturally reconstruct the jaw area hidden by the supporting hand in Image 1. Preserve the current buzz cut with absolutely no quiff, curls or raised hair volume. Do not reuse the swept hairstyle from Image 2.
